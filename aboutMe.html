WHO AM I?

CINEMATOGRAPHER

FILMMAKER.

Absolute Cinema

I'm a 19 year old cinematographer based in Indonesia, currently pursuing my university studies while actively working on film and event projects.

My work spans narrative films, documentaries, church productions, and commissioned projects. Through each experience, I've developed a disciplined approach to cinematography focusing on structured composition, controlled lighting, and storytelling that supports the subject rather than overpowering it.
